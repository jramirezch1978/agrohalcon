All data, resources and external tools used in AllpaShift, with their licenses.

NASA data

NASA Langley Research Center. POWER Project (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources), Agroclimatology community, API v2. Open data. https://power.larc.nasa.gov/

Huffman, G. J., et al. GPM IMERG Final Precipitation L3 1 month 0.1° x 0.1° V07 (GPM_3IMERGM). NASA GES DISC. https://doi.org/10.5067/GPM/IMERG/3B-MONTH/07

Huffman, G. J., et al. GPM IMERG Final Precipitation L3 1 day 0.1° x 0.1° V07 (GPM_3IMERGDF). NASA GES DISC. https://doi.org/10.5067/GPM/IMERGDF/DAY/07

Huffman, G. J., et al. GPM IMERG Late Precipitation L3 1 day 0.1° x 0.1° V07 (GPM_3IMERGDL). NASA GES DISC. https://doi.org/10.5067/GPM/IMERGDL/DAY/07

O'Neill, P. E., et al. SMAP Enhanced L3 Radiometer Global Daily 9 km EASE-Grid Soil Moisture, Version 6 (SPL3SMP_E). NASA NSIDC DAAC. https://doi.org/10.5067/M20OXIZHY3RJ

Didan, K. MODIS/Terra Vegetation Indices 16-Day L3 Global 250m SIN Grid V061 (MOD13Q1). NASA LP DAAC. https://doi.org/10.5067/MODIS/MOD13Q1.061 — accessed through the ORNL DAAC MODIS Web Service, https://modis.ornl.gov/

JPL MUR MEaSUREs Project. GHRSST Level 4 MUR Global Foundation Sea Surface Temperature Analysis (v4.1). NASA PO.DAAC. https://doi.org/10.5067/GHGMR-4FJ04

NOAA CoastWatch. ERDDAP, used to access the MUR SST subset (jplMURSST41, jplMURSST41anom1day). U.S. Government public domain. https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/

Other sources

INDECI, National Civil Defense Institute of Peru. Reports on the damage of the 2017 coastal El Niño in Piura. https://www.gob.pe/indeci

FAO. ECOCROP, crop temperature and water requirements, and Global Agro-Ecological Zones (GAEZ v4), used as reference for the crop catalog. https://gaez.fao.org/

DRAP Piura / MIDAGRI, Peru. Regional agricultural statistics: sown area by crop and valley, used for the catalog of zones, crops and common rotations. https://www.gob.pe/midagri

Poggio, L., et al. (2021). SoilGrids 2.0: producing soil information for the globe with quantified spatial uncertainty. SOIL 7, 217–240. https://doi.org/10.5194/soil-7-217-2021 — ISRIC REST API, CC-BY 4.0, https://rest.isric.org

Allen, R. G., Pereira, L. S., Raes, D., Smith, M. (1998). Crop evapotranspiration (FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56): root depth and crop coefficients. https://www.fao.org/4/x0490e/x0490e00.htm

Ayers, R. S., Westcot, D. W. (1985). Water quality for agriculture (FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 29): crop salt tolerance. https://www.fao.org/4/t0234e/t0234e00.htm

NOAA Climate Prediction Center. Weekly Niño indices, used to cross-check MUR SST. https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/data/indices/

OpenStreetMap contributors. Map data and place search (Nominatim). ODbL license, with attribution on the map. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
